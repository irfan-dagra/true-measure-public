True Measure

Privacy Policy

The short version: True Measure collects nothing.

What the game collects

Nothing. True Measure has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or gameplay data. There is nothing to sell — we never have it.

What stays on your device

Your progress — your current reign, your completed reigns, and your settings — is saved only on your own device, so the game can resume where you left off. It never leaves your device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Episode downloads

From time to time the game may download new episode files from our website so new chapters can reach you without an app update. These are one-way downloads of game content, verified for integrity before use. Nothing about you or your play is sent in return.

Feedback by email

The ending screen offers a "Share your thoughts" button. It opens your own mail app with a message addressed to us, pre-filled with a few questions. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you wrote, and we use it only to read and respond to your feedback.

Children

True Measure is designed for players aged 13 and over. Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@truemeasuregame.com
