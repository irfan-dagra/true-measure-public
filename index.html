True Measure

Chosen, not crowned.

You are the Young Warden, picked by a council of the valley's wisest to rule in a time of siege and scarcity. There are no right answers here — only competing duties, and the price each one exacts. Rule well, and the valley endures. Let any one duty fall, and your reign falls with it.

The Weight of Rule

Four duties. One measure.

Every decision you make strengthens one duty and strains another. Your reign is the balance between them.

Army

The valley's shield. Walls, watchmen, and the strength to hold the pass when trouble comes.

Treasury

The valley's store. Grain, coin, and the reserves that turn a hard winter into a survived one.

People

The valley's heart. Farmers, families, and elders whose trust is the only mandate you hold.

Word

The valley's honour. The promises you keep — to allies, to rivals, and to your own council.

If any pillar falls to zero, your reign collapses — no matter how strong the other three stand.

How It Plays

A reign in three movements

No tutorials in lecturing, no answers marked right or wrong. You learn to rule the way rulers do — by deciding, and living with it.

First

Receive a dilemma

A petitioner kneels. A captain waits. A merchant knocks. Each card brings a real problem from a living valley — a siege to prepare for, a drought to survive, a promise in conflict with an empty vault.

Second

Weigh the stakes

Listen to your advisors, who tell you what they know — never what to do. Spend a rare whisper if you must, to see the stakes more clearly. Then choose, knowing every choice feeds one pillar and starves another.

Third

Live with it

The valley remembers. A mercy shown in spring may save you in winter; a debt unpaid will find you. Fifty decisions make one reign — and at its end, the chronicle reads back the ruler you became.
